✡ Welcome to Jewbuntu Classic

Jewbuntu Classic gives a computer from the 2000s a second life: Ubuntu 7.10 with the Jewbuntu look, Hebrew typing, ShulGo for minyan times, and a web browser that still works on today's internet.

Browsing the web today: use Lynx

Most websites now require modern encryption (TLS 1.2 or newer). Firefox 2 from 2007 can't speak it, so it will fail on most sites. Lynx, in the dock and under Applications → Internet, is a current version (2.9) built for this system with up-to-date encryption. It shows pages as text: fast, light, and good for news, Wikipedia and search.

Keys: arrows move between links, Enter follows a link, Left goes back, g opens an address, / searches the page, q quits.

Typing Hebrew

Press Alt+Shift to switch between English and Hebrew. The keyboard indicator on the top bar (USA or Isr) shows which one is active.

ShulGo

When ShulGo is installed, its icon sits on the top bar next to the clock and shows the next minyan near you. Click it to choose your place and link your ShulGo account.

Please keep in mind

Ubuntu 7.10 stopped receiving security updates in 2009. It's fine for writing, reading, learning and light browsing, but don't use it for banking or anything private, and keep it behind a home router. Your own files are safest on a USB stick as well.
